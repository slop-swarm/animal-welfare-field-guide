# Review checklist

Before publishing an evidence note, check:

- [ ] Population, geography, and time horizon are explicit.
- [ ] Welfare outcomes are animal-centered; proxies are labeled, linked to predicted outcomes, and supported by evidence for that link.
- [ ] Severity, duration, and time basis (daily, cycle, or lifetime) are explicit; mortality and serious injury are considered, including culling and survivor bias.
- [ ] Sources are linked, dated, and accurately represented; uncertainty is visible.
- [ ] Evidence against the claim and relevant alternatives are considered.
- [ ] The causal chain from action to animal experience is stated and examined.
- [ ] Scale, relevant lifetime window, adoption, compliance, and counterfactual assumptions are explicit.
- [ ] Cross-species comparisons state sentience and moral-weight assumptions and test sensitivity where material.
- [ ] Trade-offs and possible displacement are discussed; the substitute destination/system and its animal welfare outcomes are considered.
- [ ] Estimates use ranges or sensitivity analysis when appropriate.
- [ ] Review date and evidence that would prompt an update are included.
